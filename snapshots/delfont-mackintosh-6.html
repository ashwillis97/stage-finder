Into The Woods

Into The Woods is the 2026 Olivier award-winning Best Musical Revival, now playing a strictly limited season at the Noël Coward Theatre.

The renowned masterpiece from Stephen Sondheim and James Lapine, reimagined by director Jordan Fein (Fiddler on the Roof) and designer Tom Scutt (Cabaret at the Kit Kat Club), Into The Woods is ‘London’s best musical in years’ (Tatler).

This joyful, witty and beautiful new production has been hailed 'triumphant and other-worldly’ (Radio Times). Now back in London with an extraordinary cast, this ‘magical production is even better than ever’ The Stage.

We all know how the story begins. But what happens after it ends?

Once upon a time… The Baker and his wife. A Witch with a curse. A boy and his cow. A girl in a red cloak. A maiden with long flowing hair. A servant covered in cinders. All journey deep into the dark woods.

To sell the cow

To visit mother

To go to the festival

To lift the spell

As the adventure unfolds and paths entwine, wishes are made, slippers are lost, beanstalks take flight and new friendships are forged – revealing that life beyond ‘happily ever after’ can still be full of magic, even when there’s darkness.

Joining Kate Fleetwood are Simbi Akande (Cinderella), Valda Aviks (Grandmother/Giant), Geoffrey Aymer (Cinderella’s Father), John Dagleish (Baker), Fiona Finsbury (Rapunzel), Jennifer Hepburn (Cinderella’s Stepmother and Alternate Witch), Hana Ichijo (Lucinda), Julie Jupp (Jack’s Mother), Lucas Koch (Rapunzel’s Prince), Gabrielle Lewis-Dodson (Florinda), Gracie McGonigal (Little Red Ridinghood), Laura Pitt-Pulford (Baker’s Wife), Jack Quarton (Steward), Keith Ramsay (Jack), Matthew Seadon-Young (Cinderella’s Prince/Wolf), and Michael Siegel (Narrator), with Hollie Aires, Sabrina Aloueche, Jacob Fowler, Adam Robert Lewis, Sophie Linder-Lee, Toby Turpin, and Elsie Watson completing the cast.

Into The Woods is presented by London Theatre Company in association with Jamie Wilson Productions

Don’t miss this landmark revival, only until 9 January.

Age Recommendation: 12+

Everyone, regardless of age, must have their own ticket to enter the theatre. Children under the age of 16 must be accompanied by, and seated next to, a ticketholder who is at least 18 years old. Children under the age of 3 will not be admitted.

Please note: This production contains haze, loud noises, flashing lights, as well as strobe effects that may affect audience members who are sensitive to light.

There will be periods of absolute darkness in the auditorium as part of the show, we ask you to remain in your seats during this time.
