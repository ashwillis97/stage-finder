Questions

When does pantomime season start?

Most pantomimes open in late November or early December and run until early January. Some start earlier and a few run into February, so check the dates for each show.

How do I find a pantomime near me?

Pick your city from the list on this page, or use the search and Where filter on the Find a Show homepage to see pantomimes at venues near you.

Are there relaxed or accessible performances?

Many venues add relaxed, signed or accessible performances to their pantomime run. They usually appear as separate listings or on the show's own page, so check with the venue.

How do I book tickets?

Use the button on each listing. It takes you to the venue or its ticket partner, where you can pick a date and seats.
