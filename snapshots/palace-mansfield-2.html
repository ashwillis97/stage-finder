  • Young Performers Open Mic - November 2026

    The Young Performers Open Mic is back this November and we’re now open to performers aged up to 25! Whether you’re a Poet, writer or singer song writer we want to hear from you! Join us on Monday 9th November at 6pm to 8pm at The Old Library, Leeming Street, to see a collection of Mansfield’s amazing emerging talent. If you’re interested in performing, please complete the sign-up form https://www.mansfield.gov.uk/xfp/form/1352 before Monday 26th October 2026 (Performers must be aged 13 to 25).     Date: 9th November 2026

  • A Celtic Christmas

    Celebrate the magic of the season with A Celtic Christmas - a spellbinding concert filled with soaring vocals, haunting Celtic instruments and breathtaking harmonies. Experience beloved Christmas classics including Joy to the World, The Holly and the Ivy, We Three Kings and Jingle Bells, beautifully reimagined with a distinctive Celtic twist. Heartwarming, uplifting and unforgettable, A Celtic Christmas is the perfect festive treat for the whole family and a wonderful way to make this Christmas truly special.

    Date: 11th November 2026

  • The Ultimate MOD Revue

    A blistering, high-voltage, feel-good show! Brought to you by the team that brought you Quadrophenia - the album, LIVE! in 2018. Featuring the very best of sing-along Mod hits with a big sound, performed by some of the UK’s finest musicians and singers - The Goldhawks! Backed by a projection of bespoke film reflecting the times and trends of the era which underscores and punctuates the music to powerful effect...let us take you back in time. This brand new show for 2025 takes you on a journey of the Mod subculture favourites from 60s through to the 90s with huge hits from The Who, The Jam, Small Faces, Ocean Colour Scene, The Kinks and many more. There are plenty of theatre shows devoted to sixties music, however, none devoted to the energy-driven Mod genre. The show is a concentrated celebration of some of the world’s most well known hits which get people singing and dancing in the aisles with wild abandon, leaving at the end of the night with that feel-good factor of time and money well spent.

    Date: 12th November 2026

  • Christmas Memories

    Neil Sands and his wonderful cast return with a brand-new production of their must-see Christmas Cracker! Get ready for a magical afternoon packed with festive joy and heartwarming nostalgia, featuring over 60 timeless Christmas songs and carols you know and love.     Date: 13th November 2026

  • Cinderella

    You shall go to the ball with 2026's sparkling pantomime, Cinderella! Grab your friends, family and colleagues, sparkle your slippers and book your tickets before the clock strikes! Stepping into the iconic crystal slippers is Emmerdale favourite Rosie Bentham, best known for her role as Gabby Thomas. Since joining the long-running ITV soap in 2016, she has become a recognisable face on UK television, earning praise for her engaging and authentic performances. Viewers have watched her character evolve through challenging storylines and her ability to portray the complexities of teenage life and its struggles have earned her admiration. A proud Nottingham native, Rosie returns close to home to bring warmth, charm and a touch of fairytale magic to this rags-to-riches tale. Cinderella’s wicked stepsisters treat her as their servant. She’s going to need all the magic her fairy godmother can muster if she is to meet her Prince Charming and find true love before midnight. With a script by Jon Monie (writer of this year’s production of Snow White & the Seven Dwarfs and winner of Best Script at the Great British Pantomime Awards 2019), this spectacular production will delight with its enchanting story, side-splitting comedy, lavish settings and adorable miniature ponies. Featuring Mansfield’s favourite funny man, Adam Moss, this year’s sparkling Cinderella also welcomes back some other familiar faces. By popular demand, Joseph Dockree returns as Prince Charming, while the unstoppable double act of Jamie Morris and Tarot Joseph are back to cause chaos as the fabulously outrageous Ugly Sisters. Niamh Hendron is also returning to Mansfield for another Christmas season – this time armed with a wand, a generous sprinkling of magic and, of course, a very important pair of glass slippers, as the enchanting Fairy Godmother. Don’t wait for your invitation – book your tickets early and make sure the whole family has a ball this Christmas. With more fun than you can shake a pumpkin at, Cinderella is the perfect festive treat!

    Date: 21st November 2026 - 3rd January 2027

  • Spotlight: Festive Performance Skills (Ages 8 to 16)

    Spotlight is an exciting drama programme for home educated young people aged 8-16. Through games, improvisation, movement, character work, script exploration and performance, participants will develop confidence, creativity, communication skills and friendships in a supportive and inclusive environment. Led by experienced drama practitioner Stacey Moon-Tracy, sessions encourage self-expression while introducing key theatre and performance techniques. This session will focus on festive performance skills, young people will have the opportunity to create their own festive sketches and then share them back with others in the class to improve their confidence skills.

    Date: 14th December 2026

  • Puss in Boots

    This year, Westfield Folkhouse presents the pantomime Puss in Boots. Will is the son of the village postmistress. He is poor but happy and always willing to help. But his life turns round in the most unexpected manner when he befriends a stray cat which turns out to be anything but ordinary! A royal visit to the village leads to another twist in Will’s life, but everything doesn’t go according to plan. And questions arise. Just who does live in the old house on the hill? And why is there more to the village cobbler than first appears? As with all Folkhouse pantomimes, this will be family entertainment of the highest order. There’s singing, dancing, comedy and audience participation as the story unfolds and ultimately, everyone lives happily ever after. With prices kept the same as last year, why not treat the whole family? We look forward to seeing you! Online bookings open at 10am Monday, 2 November 2026. Tickets are available before this date from the Westfield Folkhouse Ticket Secretary on 07424890626

    Date: 16th January 2027 - 24th January 2027

  • T.Rextasy

    Marc Bolan and T.Rex were some of the most flamboyant, charismatic stars of the original glam-rock era and with Bolan's special ingredient of rock-a-bBoogie songs, the band had a string of huge hits throughout the 1970s. T.Rextasy have performed around the world for over 20 years and the band is now accepted as the world’s only official live tribute band dedicated to T.Rex. They are the only band endorsed and approved by Marc Bolan’s family, estate, original ex-members of T.Rex, and Bolan’s catalogue management. The band has been described by many as ‘beyond the boundaries of tribute’.

    Date: 10th February 2027

  • Big Girls Don't Cry

    The internationally-acclaimed tribute to Frankie Valli & The Four Seasons. They skyrocketed to worldwide stardom 60 years ago with number one hit Sherry – so, come out tonight as we celebrate all of their million-selling hits. Walk Like a Man, Rag Doll, Oh What a Night, Silence is Golden... in one incredible concert show! So, come out tonight as we take you to a place where the moon shines bright and we dance the night away, taking you back in time with Big Girls Don’t Cry, celebrating the iconic sounds of Frankie Valli & The Four Seasons. This non-stop hit machine, authentically recreated by our phenomenal cast and live band will transport you back in time to 1963 and beyond for a magical evening enjoying all your favourites. During the 60s and 70s No1 hits Sherry, December 1963 (Oh What a Night), Walk Like a Man, Rag Doll and Big Girls Don’t Cry helped propel the Four Seasons and Frankie Valli to super stardom. After selling an incredible 100 million records worldwide, there’s a Four Seasons song that shares a special place in everyone’s heart. This internationally acclaimed show authentically revives the sublime harmonies of New Jersey’s finest. It showcases Frankie’s incredible falsetto, and features his solo hits like Grease, Let’s Hang On, Working My Way Back to You, Beggin’, I’ve Got You Under My Skin, and Who Loves You. So it’s time to hang on to what we got and celebrate one of the world’s treasures in two hours of non-stop hits with Big Girls Don’t Cry! Oh what a night it will be! It’s the time, it’s the place, it’s the motion. This is a tribute show and is no way affiliated with any original artists/estates/management companies or similar shows. Promoter reserves the right to alter the programme.

    Date: 11th February 2027
