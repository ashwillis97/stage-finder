Important Information

  • Tickets for externally promoted events can only be purchased by the event promoter. We do not have access to promoter’s box office systems, do not hold any tickets and can not answer questions relating to tickets for events marketed by external promoters.
  • Events promoted by and for Morecambe Winter Gardens Preservation Trust Limited can be purchased via the event links above. 
  • By purchasing a ticket to an event at Morecambe Winter Gardens you agree to the purchase terms and conditions.
  • Our events programme is currently limited whilst renovation work continues to restore the venue back to its former glory.
  • Payment is only taken at point of purchase.

Tom Hingley Plays Inspirals & The Limes
