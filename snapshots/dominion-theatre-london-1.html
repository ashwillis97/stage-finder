Find us

Situated on the corner of Tottenham Court Road and New Oxford Street, the Dominion Theatre is ideally located, with lots of places to eat, drink and stay nearby.

We are well-connected, with lots of transport links, so recommend getting to the theatre by public transport, if possible.
