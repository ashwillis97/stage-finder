Co-op partnership

Established in rochdale in 1944, Co-op is the largest member owned consumer co-operative in the UK.

 

Co-op was founded on a set of values that describe a better, fairer way of doing business.  Today, serving members & communities is still at the heart of everything they do - these Co-op values are what Co-op Live foundations are built on.

 

As our naming rights partners, Co-op Members get first in line access to presale tickets cia the Co-op app and members always save more with exclusive member prices across Co-op food stores, legal services, funeralcare and insurance.

 

Co-op Live will conate at least £1m a year to help the Co-op foundation deluver on its youth-led strategy and Co-op vision of 'co-operating for a fairer world'.  This collective effort is known as 'gigs that give back'.  Emphasising our shared communities to making a positive impact.

For the planet
We're putting the planet first. At Co-op Live we're committed to sustainability, from solar panels on the roof to harvesting rainwater to flush our toilets and water our plants. That's not all either, so discover exactly what we are putting in place to reach our carbon neutral aim.
For the people
As a proud member of the Manchester community, we're working closely with local people, businesses and talent. We're creating jobs for local residents, and giving £1m back to good causes across the UK through the Co-op Foundation. Explore what else we are up to.
For everyone
As a joint venture between Oak View Group and City Football Group, Harry Styles and Co-op, our mission is to change the way business is done within the live entertainment industry. Discover just who we are and what we believe in.
