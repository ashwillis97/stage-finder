Questions

What kinds of Christmas shows are there?

Carol and choral concerts, Christmas musicals, festive comedy, ballet, family shows and tribute acts all appear here, alongside well-known stories such as A Christmas Carol.

How do I find Christmas shows near me?

Pick your city from the list on this page, or use the search and Where filter on the Find a Show homepage.

When should I book?

Popular Christmas dates, especially weekends in December, can sell out. If a date you want is available, booking early is the safest option.

Where do the dates come from?

Dates are taken from each venue's own website and refreshed every day. Always check with the venue before you travel.
