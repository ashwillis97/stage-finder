About us

As the largest independent theatre in the West End, Shaftesbury Theatre takes pride in being distinctive, open and original. The Shaftesbury believes in welcoming all communities into the building and seeks to present work of the widest appeal in an ever-improving physical environment where our audience is both challenged and entertained.
