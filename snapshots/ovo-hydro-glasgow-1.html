About

When you visit any website, it may store or retrieve information on your browser, mostly in the form of cookies. This information might be about you, your preferences or your device and is mostly used to make the site work as you expect it to. The information does not usually directly identify you, but it can give you a more personalised web experience. Because we respect your right to privacy, you can choose not to allow some types of cookies. Click on the different category headings to find out more and change our default settings. However, blocking some types of cookies may impact your experience of the site and the services we are able to offer.

Where can I find timings for the event I’m attending?

Timings are generally only confirmed 2-3 days before the event and will be posted on the event page as soon as they’re confirmed. Use the search function at the top of the page (magnifying glass icon) to find the page for your event and keep checking there until timings are confirmed.

How do I book accessible tickets?

Customers who wish to buy tickets for accessible positions can do so online via our websites (ticketing powered by Ticketmaster), or by using our accessible booking line on 0141 576 3230 (open Mon-Fri 9am-2pm).

How do I get access to the OVO Live presale?

All OVO customers can sign up to OVO Live for free to enjoy presale ticket access for shows at the OVO Hydro. Plus the chance to enjoy OVO Lounges, a dedicated entrance, and customer competitions, upgrades and exclusive events.
