Answers

  1. 1
    How can I get a refund on my booking?
    Refunds are solely at the discretion of the partner organisation leading the class. For example, if it’s a Dance West Class you are requesting a refund from, the decision will be made by Dance West. The Lyric and its partners first option is to provide credit to your account for the class booking if attendance cannot be made, this credit can then be used to book classes or theatre tickets again in the future.
  2. 2
    Does my child need to be a Young Lyric member in order to book a class?
    No, classes are open to all young people and you do not need to sign up as a member with us. However, if you wanted to be a Young Lyric Member and receive £5 tickets to main house shows, to learn about performance opportunities and more, sign up on our website or fill in a physical form in the Reuben Foundation Wing foyer.
  3. 3
    What do I do if my child can’t attend a class?
    Please let the Young Lyric Host know on 07923616207. They will inform the class organiser.

Featured Answers

We do not have permanent bag storage facilities and, as such, we discourage audience members from bringing large items to the theatre.

All air brought into the auditorium is supplied at roof level before passing through a filter system.  This helps us guarantee that the air you’ll breathe is as clean as it can be.

Programmes are available to buy when you attend and pre-purchase with your tickets, for collection from a designated point on the performance date.

We are cashless at the Lyric Bar, Outsider Tart @ the Lyric, and at the Box Office. We accept contactless and chip-and-pin payment.

For lost property enquires, please send a description of the item and where you last saw it in the building to enquiries@lyric.co.uk and we’ll let you know if we’ve found it. Please note that items are only kept for a 14 day period.

The Lyric welcomes babies to be breastfed in any public area of these premises.

The Lyric has a range of toilet facilities available including all-gender, gender specific and private accessible toilets in our Bar, on the Stalls level and on our balcony level near the Creative Space.

We also have a baby change facility and accessible toilet on our first floor near the entrance to the Reuben Foundation Wing.

We cannot guarantee entry to latecomers so please plan to arrive in good time before the show starts. No refunds or exchanges will be offered in the event that you arrive late.

The Lyric Hammersmith Theatre reserves the right to deny access to the theatres to any latecomers, unless a suitable point in the production has been deemed appropriate. If we are able to seat you at a pre-arranged latecomers point, please note that we will not be able to guarantee access to the exact seats booked.

Audience members who leave the auditorium once the performance has started may be asked to wait until an appropriate moment in the performance to be re-admitted. If there is no appropriate moment or admittance will cause disruption, re-admittance may be refused. No refund or exchange will be offered in this instance.

All tickets – whether purchased online, on the phone or in person – will have been issued as e-tickets sent from our tickets@lyric.co.uk address.

These will have been emailed to you at the time of booking. You can also find your tickets by logging into your account above.

If we have your mobile number on file, you will also receive a text a day before you visit with a link to download your tickets.

We are unable to offer a refund on your tickets if you are no longer able to attend. If a performance is going ahead as originally advertised, once payment has been received, all tickets are non-refundable.

We will exchange tickets for another performance of the same production on a different date/time, subject to availability. You must contact the Lyric Box Office at least 24 hours before your performance time. There is a £2 admin fee for this service.

If you are unable to attend a performance and cannot exchange for another date/time, we can exchange the value of your tickets for credit to be redeemed against future Lyric Hammersmith Theatre productions. You will need to contact the Box Office at least 24 hours before your performance time.  Credit will be valid for a year from issue date, and any credit not used within that time period will be assumed to be a donation to the Lyric.

If a performance is sold out and you are no longer able to attend the performance, please send an email to tickets@lyric.co.uk to discuss the possibility of putting your ticket up for re-sale. A re-sale is not guaranteed but if successful, the total cost of the tickets, minus a £2 admin fee, is returned to you via your original payment method. Please note this may take up to 5 working days to process.

All standard ticket purchases include a mandatory restoration levy of £2 per basket.

The restoration levy goes directly towards essential building maintenance and the upkeep of our 130 year old auditorium.

The restoration levy is waived for those booking access tickets, for schools bookings, and for Young Lyric members including Young Lyric classes.

If a performance is sold out, please send an email to tickets@lyric.co.uk if you would like to be added to a waiting list for returns. If any tickets are returned before 10am on the day of the performance, those on the waiting list will be contacted first for the opportunity to purchase.

The Lyric Hammersmith Theatre reserves the right to refuse the ticket holder admission to a performance if the ticket has been sold for commercial gain. This includes tickets sold via a secondary selling site (e.g. StubHub, Viagogo, eBay, Twickets).

The recommended age for each show changes from performance to performance. Please do double check on the page of the show that you’re interested in attending to confirm the age guidance.

Under 14s must be accompanied by and seated with a full paying adult (18+).

Under 5s will not be admitted to a show unless specified in the age guidance, with the exception of Chilled Performances which are suitable for parents/carers with babes-in-arms. Such performances are clearly signposted at the point-of-sale.

For children’s performances, all children over the age of 18 months must have a valid ticket. Those under 18 months are admitted as babes-in-arms.

Young Lyric classes are a range of creative after school and holiday classes for young people to take part in. Classes include from theatre, dance, music, and disability arts. They are led by the Lyric and its partner organisations, and all take place at the Lyric Hammersmith Theatre.

Each specific class will have an age range that’s applicable e.g. 7 – 9 year olds.  We have classes available from age 3 up; you can see the age range of each specific class on the class page.

Classes take place every school term for 10 weeks, there are no classes during half term.

We provide bursaries that alleviate the whole cost of a class. This lasts for one term, you will need to reapply for every term you/your child would like to return. For specific classes, we have a limited number of bursary places available which get filled up very quickly, you can check on the specific website class page to see if a bursary is applicable to that class- bursary applications open when a class is on sale, and all bursary holders for that current term will be emailed to reapply. Bursaries are prioritised to those who live within the top 50% of deprived wards in West London as a stipulation of the funding that supports them.

You can buy Classes online, over the phone or in person. The majority of classes are only available to book ‘per term’ as opposed to ‘per class’ however specific classes may offer bookings per class or as trial space. Each specific class webpage will have more information if booking ‘per class’ is available!
