  • Mercury

    For over a quarter century, Mercury has risen to the top, achieving premier status as the authentic Freddie Mercury and Queen Production to see in 2025. This award-winning and fully revitalised stage production will take audiences on a rapturous ride through timeless tunes with faithfully accurate portrayals and performances of the legendary QUEEN. Playing the world’s most flamboyant frontman, Joseph Lee Jackson brings the heat with incredible charisma and the extraordinary vocal range required to pay proper tribute to the great Freddie Mercury. Joseph says: “It is an absolute honour to perform as Freddie and we are delighted that Freddie’s family see us as an authentic Queen tribute.” Backed by the trademark stack of Vox AC30s, Glenn Scrimshaw coaxes the iconic rocking riffs, channelling the melodic genius of Brian May in distinctive style. The band provides impossibly tight harmonies and venue-moving beats for an unforgettable night of music. All the hits are here including the most popular Queen tunes such as Bohemian Rhapsody, Radio Ga Ga, We Are the Champions, We Will Rock You and many more. This state-of-the-art production will captivate die-hard Queen fans who were there the first time around and new generations of rock fans alike.

    Date: 23rd October 2026

  • Turner & Constable - Exhibition On Screen

    Witness a clash of fire and water at the Mansfield Palace Theatre Lounge. This event invites artists, creatives, and culture enthusiasts to connect and relax. This Week’s Film: Turner & Constable Celebrating the 250th anniversary of their births, this unmissable new documentary explores the lives, rivalry, and legacies of Turner and Constable alongside Tate Britain’s groundbreaking exhibition. Born within a year of each other, Turner and Constable were fierce rivals who transformed landscape painting. Turner’s blazing sunsets and sublime visions of travel stood in stark contrast to Constable’s nostalgic depictions of the English countryside. Their differing approaches – compared at the time to a clash of “fire and water” – whipped audiences into a frenzy of admiration and debate. With exclusive access to the exhibition, the film offers intimate views of sketchbooks, personal items, and rarely seen works. Leading experts share fresh insights into both artists, revealing unexpected sides to their lives and practices, and showing how together they reshaped British art. Why Attend? Experience Tate Britain’s landmark exhibition from your cinema seat. Explore the rivalry and brilliance of Turner and Constable, side by side. Gain unique insights through exclusive access to personal items, sketchbooks, and expert commentary. Enjoy tea, coffee, and biscuits while connecting with fellow art lovers in an intimate setting. Date: Friday 13 November 2026 Tickets: £12.00 Certificate: U

  • Spotlight: Improvisation & Creativity (Ages 8 to 16)

    Spotlight is an exciting drama programme for home educated young people aged 8-16. Through games, improvisation, movement, character work, script exploration and performance, participants will develop confidence, creativity, communication skills and friendships in a supportive and inclusive environment. Led by experienced drama practitioner Stacey Moon-Tracy, sessions encourage self-expression while introducing key theatre and performance techniques. This session will focus on improvisation and creativity, young people will focus on how to build stories, create characters and scenes on the spot.
