AXS Our Trusted Ticketing Partner

AXS is the official ticketing partner for the first direct bank arena Leeds, providing enhanced ticketing experiences for all customers, whilst also helping to prevent touts and inflated ticket prices.

How to secure tickets via AXS.com

Offering a streamlined ticket-buying process for customers by utilising the user-friendly AXS platform, specifically designed to make purchasing tickets easier and smoother than ever before. With the most up to date version of the app, fans will be able to access their account quickly via biometric login, conveniently and securely using face or touch identification.

 

Can I upgrade my experience?

Already got your ticket?

No worries we have got you covered. 

Each show has its own upgrade option, this includes early entry and a welcome drink. Some of our upgrades even include a 2 or 3 course meal. 

Its never too late! Look out for our on the night upgrades! 

Find out more about upgrades

 

How do I find out the timings for my event?

We always try to include the most up to date information to the event pages on the website, but timing information can change.

Please arrive in plenty of time to avoid disappointment and to get through security and ticket checks.
