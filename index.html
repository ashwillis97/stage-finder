    Privacy and about

    Find a Show is an independent listings guide. It is not run by or affiliated with the theatres and venues listed. Each "View show" button takes you to the venue's own page, where you can book.

    No accounts or tracking. We don't ask you to sign up and we don't use advertising or analytics cookies on this site.

    Your location. If you use "Near me", your location or postcode is used in your browser to work out distances. A postcode is looked up with postcodes.io, and your location is never stored by us.

    Affiliate links. Some ticket buttons, such as "Buy on Ticketmaster", are affiliate links. If you buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you, and the ticket seller and its partners may set cookies when you click through.

    Suggestions. If you suggest a venue or show, the text you type is sent to us through Google Forms. We don't ask for your name or email. This page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts.
