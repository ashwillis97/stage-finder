Princess Theatre, Torquay

The Princess Theatre, Torquay is the leading live entertainment venue on The English Riviera, offering an exciting year-round programme of West End musicals, live music, comedy shows, ballet, opera, and family entertainment. From major touring productions and top UK comedians to hit concerts and a popular annual Torquay pantomime, the Princess Theatre, Torquay brings world-class performances to the South Devon coast.
