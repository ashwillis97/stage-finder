The Alban Arena

Situated in the heart of the historic market town St Albans and the largest theatre in the city, we offer a busy programme of National Touring Shows, Comedy, Rock and Pop Concerts, Films, Exhibitions and the region’s most popular Christmas Pantomime. We’ve hosted some of Britain’s best-known bands, acts and artists including, The Who, Status Quo, Morecambe and Wise, Dire Straits, The Gorillaz and Friendly Fires. Formally known as St Albans City Hall or Civic Hall, the theatre can host over 850 people seated and 1200 standing.

We’re proud to be a warm and welcoming venue, with a varied programme of events offering something for everyone. Experience the superb Alban Arena first-hand with our immersive 360° virtual tour.
