August: Osage County

One family. One reunion. Every secret laid bare.

The Pulitzer Prize and Tony Award-winning sensation, hailed as ‘the first great American play of the 21st century’ (The Guardian), August: Osage County arrives in the West End in a searing new production, starring two-time Olivier Award winner and 2026 Tony Award winner for Best Actress Lesley Manville alongside two-time BAFTA winner Anna Maxwell Martin, and directed by Jeremy Herrin whose award-winning work includes Every Brilliant Thing, People, Places and Things and Wolf Hall.

Following a sudden family crisis, the fiercely dysfunctional Weston family reunites at their Oklahoma home. As old wounds reopen and long-buried secrets come to light, simmering resentments erupt in this unfiltered portrait of family life.

By turns darkly funny and deeply moving, Tracy Letts' modern masterpiece is packed with unforgettable characters, blistering dialogue and explosive truths. A gripping theatrical experience that will make you laugh, gasp and leave you thinking long after the curtain falls.

Don't miss this landmark production in the West End.

Age Recommendation: Age 13+

Everyone, regardless of age, must have their own ticket to enter the theatre. Children under the age of 16 must be accompanied by and sat next to a ticketholder who is at least 18 years old. Children under the age of 3 will not be admitted.

Please note: This production contains strong language, depictions of drug addiction and adult themes.

Performance schedule

Evenings at 6.30pm; matinees at 1pm & 2.30pm; please check the performance calendar for more information.

Show Running Time

Approximately 3 hours including an interval

Latecomers may not be admitted until a suitable break, but we cannot guarantee admittance into the performance.
If you need to leave your seat during the performance, you may be asked to wait until a suitable moment before re-entering the auditorium. This is to minimise disturbance both to other audience members and the performers on stage.
Please note that you may not bring food or drink purchased elsewhere into our theatres.
The producers may make alterations to the published cast for any reason, at any time, and therefore cannot guarantee the appearance of any named artist.
Filming, photography and phone use are not permitted during the performance.
